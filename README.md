# FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel data analytics project developed to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and delivery operations.

The dashboard transforms raw sales transaction data into meaningful business insights using **Excel Tables, Calculated Columns, PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPI Cards, and Interactive Slicers**.

The analysis covers **1,000 orders** and approximately **₹35.21 lakh in total revenue**, providing a comprehensive view of sales performance across products, categories, occasions, months, days, and cities.

---

## Problem Statement

Businesses generate large volumes of sales data, but analyzing raw transactional data manually can make it difficult to identify important sales trends and business opportunities.

The key challenges addressed in this project are:

- Difficulty in monitoring overall sales performance.
- Lack of a centralized view of important sales KPIs.
- Difficulty identifying high-performing product categories.
- Limited visibility into monthly and daily revenue trends.
- Difficulty understanding occasion-based purchasing behavior.
- Lack of geographical analysis of customer orders.
- Difficulty evaluating customer spending and delivery performance.

This project addresses these challenges by developing an interactive Excel dashboard that converts raw sales data into actionable business insights.

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Revenue by Category

Analyzes revenue generated by different product categories and helps identify high-performing and low-performing categories.

### 2. Revenue by Month

Analyzes monthly revenue trends to identify seasonal patterns and peak sales periods.

### 3. Revenue by Day

Compares revenue across different days of the week to identify stronger sales days.

### 4. Occasion Analysis

Analyzes sales performance across different occasions, including:

- Anniversary
- Birthday
- Diwali
- Holi
- Raksha Bandhan
- Valentine's Day
- All Occasions

### 5. Top 10 Cities by Orders

Identifies cities generating the highest number of orders and provides insight into geographical customer demand.

### 6. Product Performance

Analyzes product-level sales performance to identify products contributing significantly to overall revenue.

### 7. Customer Spending

Analyzes average customer/order spending to understand purchasing behavior.

### 8. Delivery Performance

Analyzes order-delivery time to evaluate operational efficiency.

## Key Business Insights

- The dashboard contains **1,000 sales orders**.
- Total analyzed revenue is approximately **₹35.21 lakh**.
- **Colors** is one of the strongest-performing categories in the analyzed data.
- Sales revenue varies significantly across different months, indicating seasonal purchasing patterns.
- **Saturday** shows strong revenue performance among the analyzed days.
- Occasion-based analysis helps identify customer purchasing behavior around major events and celebrations.
- City-level analysis provides insights into geographical order distribution.
- Average customer spending is approximately **₹3,520.98**.
- Average order-delivery time is approximately **5.53**.
